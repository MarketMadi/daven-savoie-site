The problem

  • A general AI chat window has no idea what is in your file. You re-brief it every time.
  • Client facts get pasted into a browser tab that was never built to hold them.
  • Months later, someone asks how a piece of AI-assisted work was produced, and the record lives in browser history and memory.

What it does

  • Know where the file stands

    What's on file, what's missing, and what's due. Instantly, without waiting on a model.

  • Answers you can trace

    Every answer cites the actual document it came from, not a plausible-sounding guess.

  • Local by default

    Work stays on your machine. If a task needs outside processing, you preview and approve exactly what goes out first.

  • A record that already exists

    What informed a piece of work, and what you reviewed, is captured as you work rather than reconstructed afterward.

Who builds this

I'm an independent engineer with a background in DevOps and SRE, working across healthcare platforms, AI infrastructure, and financial infrastructure. Ten years in environments where data protection and uptime were not optional.

I built this after watching how much of a legal file's time goes to finding what you already have. One named person, accountable for the work. No anonymous team, no subcontractor you never meet.
