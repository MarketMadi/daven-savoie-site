About DAPS Services Ltd

DAPS Services Ltd is a Mississauga-based AI and automation consultancy that helps owner-operated businesses across the GTA find where they lose time and money, and fix it with practical tools.

Get clear. Then automate. Win back revenue, time, and trust.

What DAPS does

Free 15-minute AI tools assessment

We walk through how your business actually runs, from the first call to the final invoice. You get a clear picture of where hours and revenue are slipping away, with no pitch and no obligation.

A written report within 48 hours

You receive a short report naming the 3 to 7 biggest time sinks in your business, each matched to a specific fix. Every recommendation is mapped to your actual process, not generic AI advice.

Setup and implementation

If you want help putting the fixes in place, we set up the tools, connect them to what you already use, and train your team so they stick.

What makes DAPS different

Diagnosis before tools

Most AI vendors start with a product and look for a problem to attach it to. DAPS starts with how your business runs. Often the best fix is a feature in software you already pay for.

An engineer, not a salesperson

Daven has spent 10 years building and running production systems, including AI infrastructure, healthcare platforms and Bitcoin infrastructure. He trained machine learning models long before ChatGPT made AI mainstream.

Local and in person

DAPS is based in Mississauga and works with businesses across the GTA. You can meet face to face, and you deal directly with the person doing the work.

Small, fast and specific

You get 3 to 7 fixes, ranked by impact and effort, delivered in 48 hours. Quick wins come first, so you see results before committing to anything bigger.

Plain language

No jargon and no hype. If a recommendation can't be explained in a sentence, it doesn't make the list.

Frequently asked questions

Is the assessment really free?

Yes. It takes 15 minutes, and the written report is yours to keep whether or not we work together.

Do I need to understand AI?

No. You explain how your business runs, and DAPS translates that into plain-language fixes.

Will you try to sell me software?

No. Recommendations are based on what fits your business, including tools you already pay for.

What about customer or patient data?

The assessment looks at your workflows, not your records. DAPS never needs access to customer or patient files to find where time is being lost.

What does it cost after the assessment?

Any paid work is scoped after the assessment, so you know the price before you commit. You can also take the report and implement it yourself.

Do you work outside Mississauga?

Yes, anywhere in the Greater Toronto Area, in person or remotely.
